# Contributing to IPO.ONE

IPO.ONE accepts contributions under the repository MIT License. All commits
must include a Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 sign-off:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your-email@example.com>
```

Use `git commit -s` to add the sign-off. The sign-off certifies the exact terms
in `DCO`; it is not a copyright assignment or a separate contributor license
agreement.

Do not submit code, documentation, generated output, data, or dependency source
that you do not have the right to contribute. Third-party material must retain
its upstream license and notices and must be admitted through a reviewed exact
dependency rather than copied into the repository.

Product and engineering changes remain subject to `AGENTS.md`, the Product
Constitution, issue-sized scope, and the repository quality gate. A contribution
or passing test never grants deployment, signer, funds, real-value, mainnet, or
production authority.
